Undergraduate scholarships – College of Engineering

The College of Engineering provides a range of merit- and need-based scholarships to help undergraduates offset tuition, fees and educational expenses. The college awards over $1 million in scholarships each year, thanks largely to alumni and donor support. ￼

Key links (for use in responses):
	•	General UA scholarship information: https://financialaid.arizona.edu/types-of-aid/scholarships
	•	College of Engineering scholarships:
	•	Incoming freshmen: https://engineering.arizona.edu/undergrad/scholarships#incoming
	•	Continuing students: https://engineering.arizona.edu/undergrad/scholarships#continuing
	•	Native American students (AZ Native Scholars Grant): https://engineering.arizona.edu/undergrad/scholarships#nativeamerican
	•	Transfer students: https://engineering.arizona.edu/undergrad/scholarships#transfers

https://financialaid.arizona.edu/scholarshipuniverse ￼

Incoming first-year students
	•	Apply through Scholarship Universe to be considered for a broad set of awards with a single application.
	•	Honors College admits may also qualify for Honors Scholarships.
	•	Need-based scholarships require a FAFSA on file with UA’s Office of Scholarships and Financial Aid. ￼

Example College of Engineering awards include:
	•	Engineering Scholar Awards
	•	Thomas R. Brown Distinguished Scholarships ￼

Continuing students
	•	Current engineering students submit the General Engineering Scholarship Application via Scholarship Universe to be considered for 200+ department and college-level scholarships.
	•	Application details and deadlines typically appear in February and close mid-March.
	•	Students are encouraged to:
	•	Update résumés with UA Career Services support
	•	Refine personal statements with help from the UA Writing Skills Improvement Program ￼

Arizona Native Scholars Grant
	•	Undergraduates from any of Arizona’s 22 federally recognized Native American tribes may be eligible for free tuition and mandatory fees on the Tucson campus through the Arizona Native Scholars Grant.
	•	Details and related Indigenous student support programs:
https://financialaid.arizona.edu/types-of-aid/indigenous-wildcats ￼

Transfer students
	•	Transfer students make up roughly 20% of the incoming class each year.
	•	The college offers transfer-specific scholarships; details are on the Transfer Admissions page:
https://engineering.arizona.edu/undergrad-admissions/transfer#scholarships ￼

Math placement & major declaration (undergraduate context)

Declaring a major in Engineering
	•	Incoming first-year students usually start as “Engineering, No Major Selected” until they meet requirements to declare their specific major.
	•	ENGR 102 is an exploratory course that helps students compare majors and choose the right fit.
	•	The major declaration process is non-competitive—there are no caps or quotas. ￼

Re-evaluations and changes:
	•	Students initially admitted to non-engineering majors can request re-evaluation for the College of Engineering using a dedicated form (link in the source).
	•	There are separate processes for:
	•	Students not initially accepted to Engineering
	•	Students who did not select Engineering on their application but later want to switch
	•	Current UA students who wish to declare an engineering major ￼

Math placement guidance for #ENGRCats
	•	Math and science are foundational for all engineering majors.
	•	Students should:
	•	Submit ACT/SAT scores if available (to help determine initial math placement).
	•	Take the ALEKS PPL math placement exam early and allow time for review/retest.
	•	Consider AP or dual-enrollment coursework that might offer credit or influence placement.
	•	The College provides additional math resources and study guides via the Math Department and Engineering websites (links in the source). ￼

Graduate funding overview

The College of Engineering and UA Graduate College offer multiple funding mechanisms for MS and PhD students, including: ￼
	•	Graduate assistantships (GAs, RAs, TAs) through departments
	•	Fellowships and scholarships from internal and external sources
	•	Research and teaching positions

Key resources:
	•	Engineering funding opportunities: https://engineering.arizona.edu/current/funding-opportunities
	•	Graduate College financial resources (federal, state and institutional aid):
http://grad.arizona.edu/funding/financial-resources/federal-state-and-institutional-aid
	•	FAFSA is required for need-based grants, loans, scholarships and work-study programs. ￼

Examples of external opportunities listed in the doc include:
	•	NASA MUREP fellowships
	•	National Academies Research Associateship Programs
	•	Sandia National Labs Master’s Fellowships
	•	NSF Graduate Research Fellowships (NSF GRFP)
	•	BIO5 internships
	•	Travel awards (e.g., Institute of the Environment) for conferences and fieldwork  ￼

Departments may also advertise RA/TA openings, and students are encouraged to contact departments directly about available assistantships.
